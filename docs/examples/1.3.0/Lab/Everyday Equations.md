# Everyday Equations

These simple equations stay literal text in Write and become typeset math in Preview.

## Distance on the trail

Distance is $d=vt$. At a steady pace, $d=4(2)=8$ gives eight kilometres in two hours.

## The long way across

A right triangle follows $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. For two equal sides, $c^2=2a^2$.

## Larger formulas
