# Formula Workshop

Formulas render in Write as well as in Preview. Click one, or focus it and press Enter, to edit its TeX below it with a live rendering. Apply (Ctrl/⌘ Enter) saves it; Escape cancels.

The circle's area is $A = \pi r^2$, and its circumference is $C = 2\pi r$. A price such as $5 and $6 stays plain text, and so does an escaped \$x$.

$$
\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3}
$$

The quadratic formula fits on one line too:

$$ x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} $$

## Try it

- Change $\pi r^2$ to $\tau r^2 / 2$ and check Preview.
- Type `$a+b$` in a new line to make a formula.
- Type `$$` on an empty line and press Enter for a display formula.
- Put a space just inside the dollars and Apply: Write explains why it cannot save that.

A deliberately broken formula stays readable: $\notACommand{x}$.
