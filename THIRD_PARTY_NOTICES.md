# Third-party notices

## Browser app

The browser contains MIT-licensed Tiptap/ProseMirror, CodeMirror, Lezer, Marked and
related packages. Full license texts for every package included by the JavaScript
bundler are generated by `npm run build` into
`web/assets/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`, which is embedded in the binary and
served at `/assets/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`. These dependencies are bundled
locally, not requested from a CDN.

The server uses Goldmark (MIT), golang.org/x/sys (BSD-3-Clause), and for
cleaning HTML in the preview bluemonday (BSD-3-Clause) with its dependencies
golang.org/x/net (BSD-3-Clause), douceur (MIT) and gorilla/css
(BSD-3-Clause). Their license texts are in `docs/licenses/`.

The locally bundled Lexend variable font comes from
[Google Fonts / Lexend](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lexend).
Copyright 2018 The Lexend Project Authors. It is distributed under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1; see `docs/licenses/Lexend.txt`. The font is embedded and served
locally at `/assets/Lexend.ttf`; no font service is contacted at runtime.

## Theme palettes

Theme colors are adapted to application UI roles from the original projects:

- Ayu — https://github.com/ayu-theme/ayu-colors
- Catppuccin — https://catppuccin.com/palette/
- Nord — https://www.nordtheme.com/docs/colors-and-palettes/
- Tokyo Night — https://github.com/folke/tokyonight.nvim
- Dracula — https://github.com/dracula/dracula-theme
- Gruvbox — https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox

The app keeps the 12 named palettes' values; its Labradorite Light/Dark use
neutral surfaces with blue accents. These themes are community adaptations; they
do not imply endorsement by their original authors. The existing Go module
dependencies retain their own licenses.

## Math and diagrams

KaTeX and Mermaid are bundled under their MIT licenses, together with the
licenses for their bundled dependencies in `web/assets/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`.
Mermaid's prebundled FastDom license is also in `docs/licenses/fastdom.txt`.
Its prebundled YAML parser is replaced at build time by the patched js-yaml
package. KaTeX fonts are bundled locally under the MIT license from
[KaTeX/katex-fonts](https://github.com/KaTeX/katex-fonts); the full text is in
`docs/licenses/KaTeX-fonts.txt` and `/assets/KaTeX-fonts-LICENSE.txt`.
